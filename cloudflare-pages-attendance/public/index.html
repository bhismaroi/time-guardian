Calculation Rules

Effective · Monthly

Break Deductions

  • Monday to Thursday: 12:00 – 12:30 deducted.
  • Friday: 11:30 – 13:00 deducted.

Flexi Time

  • Flexi 1: 08:00 – 08:15 (no tardiness).
  • Flexi 2: 08:15 – 08:30 (no tardiness).
  • After 08:30: tardiness applies.

Overtime

  • Monday to Thursday: overtime starts at 17:30.
  • Friday: overtime starts at 18:00.

Data Merging

  • Earliest clock-in & latest clock-out across both sources.
  • Match by full name or first/last-name overlap.
